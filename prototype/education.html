The five pearl types we sell

All are genuine cultured pearls, grown inside a living mollusc. They differ in the water they grow in, their size, their colours and their price.

FreshwaterCultured in lakes and rivers. The widest range of shapes and natural colours (white, pink, lavender, peach) and the best value. Our everyday and gifting range.
AkoyaThe classic Japanese saltwater pearl: perfectly round, brilliant mirror lustre, 6 to 9 mm. The strand you picture when you think “pearls”.
South SeaThe largest and rarest, 9 to 16 mm, in white and natural gold. Thick nacre and a soft satin glow. Heirloom pieces.
TahitianNaturally dark pearls from French Polynesia: black, grey, peacock green, aubergine. Bold and modern.
KeshiSmall, all-nacre pearls with no bead nucleus and exceptional lustre. Free-form shapes, wonderful in layered strands.

The grade on your certificate

  • Akoya: graded A to AAA. AAA is the highest, with mirror lustre and a near-flawless surface.
  • Freshwater, South Sea and Tahitian: graded A to AAAA. AAAA is the best.
  • Our everyday freshwater range is A quality: genuine, clean and lustrous at an honest price. Our sets and heirloom strands go up to AAA and AAAA.

How to tell real pearls from fake

  • The tooth test. Rub a pearl gently against the edge of your front tooth. A real pearl feels slightly gritty; glass and plastic feel perfectly smooth.
  • Temperature. Real pearls feel cool when you first pick them up and warm slowly against the skin. Plastic is room temperature immediately.
  • Small imperfections. No two real pearls are identical. Perfectly uniform, flawless pearls at a low price are almost always imitation.
  • Look at the drill hole. On a real pearl the edge is clean and you can often see the nacre layers. On imitations the coating chips around the hole.
  • Weight. Real pearls are heavier than they look. Plastic beads feel light for their size.

Caring for your pearls

  • Last on, first off. Put pearls on after perfume, hairspray and make-up, and take them off before you undress.
  • Wipe after wearing. A soft, dry cloth removes skin oils and keeps the lustre.
  • Store separately. Pearls are softer than metal and gemstones. Keep them in the pouch we send, away from other jewellery.
  • Never soak. No ultrasonic cleaners, no chemicals, no long baths. Water weakens the silk thread.
  • Restring every one to two years if worn often. Restringing and polishing are free at any Mangatrai showroom, for life.
