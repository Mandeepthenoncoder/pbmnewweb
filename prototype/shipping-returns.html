Shipping & returns

Free, insured delivery across India and a 15-day no-questions return policy. Here is exactly how it works.

ShippingCash on deliveryReturnsExchanges

Shipping

  • Free across India. Every order, every pincode, no minimum. Customs and duties do not apply to domestic orders.
  • Dispatch within 24 hours on working days (Monday to Saturday). Customised or personalised pieces need 1 to 3 days.
  • Delivery in 4 to 7 days of dispatch for most of India; metro cities are usually faster. Enter your pincode on any product page for an estimate.
  • Insured and tracked. Shipments travel with trusted courier partners, fully insured. You receive tracking by email and SMS, and the courier calls before delivery. Track at Blue Dart.
  • Packaging. Pieces arrive in a tamper-proof Pearls by Mangatrai box with the certificate of authenticity. Gift packaging and a handwritten note are complimentary on request.
  • India only for now. We do not currently ship internationally.

Cash on delivery

  • Available on orders up to ₹20,000.
  • Please keep the exact amount ready; couriers may not carry change.
  • Refused or undelivered COD parcels attract a 10% cancellation fee plus shipping costs on any future refund.

Returns

  • 15 days from the shipping date for a full refund on orders above ₹1,000, no questions asked.
  • Tell us within 24 hours of delivery on WhatsApp, phone or email. We arrange a free pickup within 2 working days.
  • The piece must be unused, not personalised, and in original condition with its tag, certificate and invoice.
  • Refunds are issued within 7 to 10 working days of the piece reaching our warehouse, to the original payment method. COD orders are refunded by bank transfer.
  • If you send the piece back yourself rather than using our pickup, please use a tracked service; we cannot guarantee receipt otherwise. Minimum return shipping cost is around ₹250.
  • Items priced ₹1,000 or below are not eligible for refund or exchange.

Exchanges

  • Exchange within 15 days for any other piece. The full value is credited toward the new purchase; coupon or discount amounts are deducted from the credit.
  • Return shipping for exchanges is on us.
