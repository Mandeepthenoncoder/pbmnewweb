Our story

Since 1905. Five generations. One standard.

Hyderabad has trusted Mangatrai for pearls for more than 120 years. This is who we are and why every strand we sell carries our name.

It began near Charminar

In 1905, a 23-year-old named Mangatraiji opened a small store near Charminar with a simple idea: handcraft exquisite, exclusive jewellery that people would cherish when they marked the special moments of their lives.

Hyderabad was already the pearl capital of India. Mangatraiji earned his place in it by grading every pearl himself, refusing anything that did not meet his eye, and treating each customer as a relationship rather than a sale.

Five generations of the same discipline

That discipline has passed from hand to hand for five generations. Many of the artisans who string, knot and set our pearls learned the craft from their own parents in our workshops. The proprietors are still personally involved in quality checks, from the design table to the final polish.

Today Mangatrai runs 15 showrooms across Hyderabad. Pearls by Mangatrai is our dedicated pearl house, with boutiques at Liberty, Gachibowli and the Taj Falaknuma Palace, and an online store that ships free to every pincode in India.

What we promise

  • Real pearls, always. Genuine cultured freshwater, Akoya, South Sea and Tahitian pearls, never shell or glass. Every order ships with a signed certificate of authenticity.
  • Graded by hand. Lustre, surface, shape, size, colour and match, checked by people who have done nothing else for decades. See how we grade.
  • Guaranteed for life. If any laboratory ever finds our pearls are not genuine, we refund in full, with no time limit. Restringing and polishing are free at any showroom, for life.
  • Honest prices. We buy direct and sell direct. Online prices are 30% below our own showroom tags for the same pearls.
